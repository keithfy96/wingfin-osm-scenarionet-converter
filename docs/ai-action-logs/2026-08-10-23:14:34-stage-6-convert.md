## What landed

`osm-scenario convert -w WORKSPACE [--config ...]` turns `lane-model/reviewed.json` into a
map-only ScenarioNet dataset under `<workspace>/scenarionet/`: `scenario.pkl`,
`dataset_summary.pkl`, `dataset_mapping.pkl`, plus `reports/scenario-conversion.json` and a
`stage_6` manifest record. It reads the lane model and never writes it.

Gated the way Stage 5 gates Stage 4 (`validation._check_stage_4`): `stage_5.status` must be
`passed`, and `sha256(reviewed.json)` must equal what Stage 5 signed.

## The two decisions worth recording

**`entry_lanes` / `exit_lanes` hold two kinds of id, and only one is a lane.** A
continuation is the next lane's id; a junction movement is a *connector* id with the far
lane inside it. ScenarioNet takes lane ids only, so `_lane_neighbours` swaps every
connector reference for the lane it leads to. In `junction-1`: 422 lane ids passed through,
166 connector references swapped — exactly twice the 83 active connectors, which is the
arithmetic that says nothing was missed. A connector whose status is not `active` is
dropped rather than followed; `junction-1` references none, which is precisely when that
guard is cheap.

**The dataset records reachability, not Stage 5's `routing_components`.** Stage 5 reports 6
pieces using `nx.weakly_connected_components`, which ignores one-way direction. That is the
right measure for "is this map internally sound" and the wrong one for "can a route be
driven". Measured respecting direction, `junction-1` is **274** pieces — one loop of 12
lanes, 273 lanes each on their own — and only 6,556 of 80,940 lane-to-lane journeys exist
(8%). So `metadata.routing` names the best starting lane (`60444583932ec866`, way
`1351503426`) and that it reaches 79 of 285 lanes. Piece counts alone would have told
whoever runs MetaDrive later that the map is better connected than it is.

Neither is a defect in Stage 5. Every one of the six pieces reaches the edge of the extract,
so Stage 5's `isolated_component` rule correctly raises nothing; merging them would mean
downloading a larger extract, which is Keith's call, not a fix.

## What is deliberately not claimed

Signals are **counted** in metadata, never converted: their timing is not in the source, and
a fabricated phase plan is indistinguishable from a surveyed one once it is inside a pickle.
Boundaries convert as generic `ROAD_EDGE_BOUNDARY` because `boundary_type` is `unknown`
throughout the lane model.

Field names taken from the ScenarioNet format rather than from our own model cannot be
checked here — ScenarioNet is not a dependency, is not vendored, and the repo's Stage 6 spec
is prose without a field list. This is an absence of evidence, not a known defect: the
content is right, the key it is filed under is unconfirmed. All five are in
`_UNVERIFIED_FIELDS` and repeated in the conversion report so the isolated environment turns
them into one edit rather than a search.

## Found while testing, then fixed at Keith's direction

`validate-map` on a workspace with no manifest raised an uncaught `ApplyReviewError` and
printed **nothing** before exiting 1 — `_read_json` is shared from Stage 4 and its error type
is not in `validate_map_command`'s except clause. Stage 6 avoids this with a `_read` wrapper
that re-raises as `ConversionError`, pinned by
`test_convert_reports_a_stage_6_failure_as_one_line`.

Reported as pre-existing and outside the approved scope; Keith said fix it. `validation.py`
now has the same `_read` wrapper, and its three borrowed reads — the Stage 1 manifest, the
Stage 4 comparison, and the reviewed lane model — go through it. All three Stage 5 failure
paths now print a sentence:

```
Stage 5 failed: Stage 1 manifest not found: .../source/manifest.json
Stage 5 failed: Stage 1 manifest is not valid JSON: Expecting property name ...
Stage 5 failed: Stage 4 has not passed; run apply-review first
```

Pinned by `test_validate_map_reports_a_stage_5_failure_as_one_line`. `workspaces/junction-1`
re-validated afterwards: still `passed`, 0 errors, 0 warnings — behaviour on a good workspace
is unchanged, only the error path moved.
